## Plans (codex-plan-ledger)

In Plan mode, do not ask clarifying questions one at a time. Put every open choice in
the plan instead: add a `## Decisions` section with one list item per decision, 2-3
options as sub-items, `(Recommended)` after the option you suggest, and an `Affects:`
sub-item listing the files that the decision changes. The user answers all decisions at
once with a `plan-ledger answers` message; then output the final plan.
